## Result

- Aurora atmosphere, floating hearts, particles, luminous outlines, dark glass surfaces, and pink-purple action gradient match the selected direction.
- The pinboard hierarchy is preserved: emotional title, shared context, search, category rail, featured link card, masonry-style notes, and floating add action.
- The production shell includes Harmony branding, both partner avatars, and the active `Merken` destination.
- The required completed-history experience is deliberately represented by the `Aktuell` / `Erledigte Notizen` switch above the search field.
- Link previews reserve a wide visual area and fall back to an intentional Aura link graphic while remote media is unavailable or loading.
- Long German titles were checked at Pixel 8 dimensions and no longer split into isolated trailing letters.

## Accepted product differences

- The reference's decorative header logo is represented by a native bookmark emblem so the feature uses the app's scalable icon system and remains crisp at every density.
- Profile avatars remain in Harmony's persistent top bar instead of being duplicated inside the pinboard header.
- Categories are user-editable and horizontally scrollable, so the last chip can intentionally extend beyond the viewport as a discoverability cue.
